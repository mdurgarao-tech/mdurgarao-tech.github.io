~/about

About

I'm a network security engineer based in Hyderabad with close to six years of experience spanning security operations, consulting, and engineering roles. Most of my work sits where networks, proxies, and cloud meet.

Day to day that's meant administering firewall and proxy infrastructure, investigating and resolving security incidents within SLA, and running firewall changes through a clean ITIL process. More recently I've moved into Zscaler Zero Trust migrations — replacing legacy Blue Coat proxy setups with ZIA and ZPA — and extending security controls into AWS.

I like the operational side of security: fast incident resolution, disciplined change management, and automating the repetitive parts with Python so they stop eating time.
